04 / First Principles Diagnostic

The 5 Whys Root Cause Analysis

Tracing why Visakhapatnam undergraduates experience recurring academic stress back to its foundational infrastructure vacuum.

WHY 1

Why do engineering students experience severe distress the night before semester examinations?

Because they spend hours locating reliable, readable revision notes rather than actually studying the material.

WHY 2

Why can't students locate reliable revision notes in their study groups?

Because study materials are circulated as unindexed photos and unorganized PDFs buried within thousands of casual chat messages.

WHY 3

Why do students depend on casual chat groups instead of institutional college websites?

Because official college portals only host sparse textbook lists or generic lecture slides, failing to provide unit-specific student annotations or exam-tested summaries.

WHY 4

Why has nobody maintained a structured digital repository for these student annotations?

Because existing national ed-tech repositories prioritize monetization, aggressive paywalls, and pan-India generic materials that ignore local university regulations (R20, R22).

ROOT

The Root Cause:

The absence of a free, hyper-localized, syllabus-mapped peer index created by and for Visakhapatnam engineering students.

NoteVault was engineered precisely to bridge this institutional void.
