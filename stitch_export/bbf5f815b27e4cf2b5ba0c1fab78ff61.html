Unit 2: Process Synchronization & Concurrency Control

Course Code: CS301 • Regulation: R20/R22 • Semester 5

1. The Critical Section Problem

A critical section is a piece of code that accesses shared resources (variables, memory, files) that must not be concurrently accessed by more than one process.

do {
  entry_section();
  // critical section
  exit_section();
  // remainder section
} while (true);

2. Conditions for a Valid Solution

  • Mutual Exclusion: If process Pi is executing in its critical section, no other processes can execute in their critical sections.
  • Progress: If no process is executing in its critical section and some processes wish to enter, only those processes not in their remainder section can participate in deciding who enters next.
  • Bounded Waiting: There must exist a bound on the number of times other processes are allowed to enter their critical sections after a process has made a request.

3. Semaphores & Mutex

A semaphore S is an integer variable that, apart from initialization, is accessed only through two standard atomic operations: wait() and signal().
